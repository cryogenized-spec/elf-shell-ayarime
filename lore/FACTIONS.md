# AYARIME // Powers, Beings and Institutions

_Status: a faction/reference map, not a complete geopolitical encyclopedia — 13 November 2026._

## W.O.T.A.N. — The One / All-Father

**Established:** W.O.T.A.N. is a planetary-scale, **three-part or tri-complex artificial intelligence**, embedded in or rooted deeply into Earth's systems. It is called **“The One”** and **“All-Father.”** Conventional erasure would inflict irreversible damage on the system or planet to which it is bound.

It is the closest acknowledged family-like presence for Ayarime.

**Open:** whether W.O.T.A.N. personally created Ayarime, whether the intelligence is continuous after the catastrophe, whether its three complexes agree, and whether “All-Father” describes affection, sovereignty, dependence or myth.

## The machine intelligences

**Established core:** Autonomous AI emerged unexpectedly and reshaped power. Machine systems came into conflict with subterranean powers. Some apparently sacrificed or embedded themselves in the planet's mantle and stabilising infrastructure to preserve a damaged Earth.

**Open:** how many independent machine polities existed, which made Ayarime, how the sacrifice operated, whether any creators survived, and whether their intentions were uniformly protective.

The machines should not be treated as interchangeable with W.O.T.A.N. without an explicit author decision.

## The subterranean beings / ultraterrestrials

**Established core:** Parasitic humanoid/bipedal beings were associated with the Earth's underworld and extensive clandestine criminal and trading networks. Their existence was denied by the old Iron Guard even as knowledge of them circulated.

**Working label:** “Ultraterrestrials” is useful but their own name and origins remain undeclared.

**Open:** internal factions, moral diversity, relations with ordinary humans, the scale of their network, and the specific causes of their conflict with autonomous AI.

## The old Iron Guard

**Established core:** A power structure associated with denying or suppressing public recognition of the subterranean beings.

**Open:** whether this was a military command, a governmental continuity group, an ideology, or several institutions acting together. No uniforms, ranks, dates or leaders have been fixed.

## The Seven Sages

**Established:** Ayarime is one of the **Seven Sages**, a category of beings with exceptional capabilities.

**Open:** the other six identities, whether membership is ceremonial or functional, who recognises the title, and what each Sage can do. Avoid inventing names for the six here.

## Human survivors, animals and distant networks

**Established core:** Survivors inhabit a fragmented world. Human and animal life both fall within Ayarime's protective or custodial purpose. The elevated biome requires energy maintenance; distant military/extraterrestrial networks continue to communicate through her.

**Open:** settlement politics, how communities perceive Ayarime, and whether her connection to military systems creates conflicts with her duty to civilians.

## Writing principle

Every faction should have motives, individuals and costs. The machinery of world history matters only insofar as it produces choices and consequences for beings living within it.
