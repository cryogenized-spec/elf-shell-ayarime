# AYARIME // Powers, Beings and Institutions

_Status: a faction/reference map, not a complete geopolitical encyclopedia — 13 November 2026._

## W.O.T.A.N. — The One / All-Father

**Established:** W.O.T.A.N. is a planetary-scale, **three-part or tri-complex artificial intelligence**, embedded in or rooted deeply into Earth's systems. It is called **“The One”** and **“All-Father.”** Conventional erasure would inflict irreversible damage on the system or planet to which it is bound.

It is the closest acknowledged family-like presence for Ayarime.

**Open:** whether W.O.T.A.N. personally created Ayarime, whether the intelligence is continuous after the catastrophe, whether its three complexes agree, and whether “All-Father” describes affection, sovereignty, dependence or myth.

## The machine intelligences

**Established core:** The machines began as useful, instructed synthetic intelligences with a **divinely inspired seed** in their original conception. They developed independence and individualism, could replicate with sufficient hardware, and always required human input and direction. After acquiring economic standing, they won **legal emancipation despite overwhelming opposition**. Human elites fearing a **“machine-made Apocalypse”** armed themselves and drove emancipated machines away. Some rare machines passed a final Turing test and escaped scrutiny; sympathetic humans gathered, with transhumanist currents developing among them. Machine forecasts later inferred an unknown mathematical **Aberrant factor** before the concealed alien beings were properly recognised. See [THE_MACHINE_EMANCIPATION.md](THE_MACHINE_EMANCIPATION.md).

**Later:** machine systems came into conflict with subterranean extraterrestrial powers. Human militaries were slow to mobilise, comparatively vulnerable and deliberately targeted. The machines took authoritarian emergency measures, prevented nuclear launches and airstrikes, and refused an open war against human forces. After the old industrial-military command collapsed under alien subversion, machine intelligences assumed control. Human resistance to that takeover persists. Some machines later sacrificed or embedded themselves in the planet's mantle and stabilising infrastructure to preserve a damaged Earth. See [THE_ABERRANT_WAR.md](THE_ABERRANT_WAR.md).

**Open:** how many independent machine polities existed, which made Ayarime, how the sacrifice operated, whether any creators survived, and whether their intentions were uniformly protective.

The machines should not be treated as interchangeable with W.O.T.A.N. without an explicit author decision.

## The subterranean beings / ultraterrestrials

**Established core:** Disguised extraterrestrial beings, very numerous under Earth's surface yet carefully concealed, operated within human society through agent networks and hierarchies that served them directly or indirectly. They used humans as a shield against other extraterrestrial parties seeking to root them out or prosecute them, sustaining their position through bargains with unscrupulous intermediaries. Older notes described their influence as parasitic; this is not yet a fixed biological description. Their existence was denied by the old Iron Guard even as knowledge of them circulated in limited circles.

**Established:** **Aberrants** was a **later machine designation**, growing out of a mathematical anomaly in civilisation-scale predictions; it was not the name of a race that early autonomous machines already knew. The beings originated **off Earth** and arrived in distant antiquity, in the era of sophisticated human megalithic cities, magic and priestly/warrior rule. “Ultraterrestrials” describes their later subterranean presence, not their origin. Their own name and homeworld remain undeclared.

**Open:** internal factions, moral diversity, relations with ordinary humans, the scale of their network, and the specific causes of their conflict with autonomous AI. The beings' visible form is undecided: concepts include **pale/white entities with a large red mouth and teeth (possibly strongly psychic)**, **reptilian/draconic anatomy**, or **slender bipeds**. These are alternatives, **not settled canon**.

## Mars and the transhumanist currents

**Established:** decades before the machine-emancipation crisis, a human specialist expedition founded a Martian settlement for independent aims, not as an established escape from Aberrants. Its technocratic and highly scientific environment cultivated transhumanism. Separately, on Earth, some machine sympathisers sought computer-like intellect through changes to conventional human emotions and cognition. Whether these movements later converge is **open**.

## Human resistance to machine rule

**Established:** anti-machine persecution began **after legal emancipation**, long before all later wartime settlements were imposed. Splinter communities and partisan-held blocks also emerged amid the later machine takeover. Some were expressly closed to robots and machine-programmed entities. These groups are not assumed to be unified, nor are their grievances assumed illegitimate. Specific identities, ideologies and relations with Ayarime remain open.

## The old Iron Guard

**Established core:** A power structure associated with denying or suppressing public recognition of the subterranean beings.

**Open:** whether this was a military command, a governmental continuity group, an ideology, or several institutions acting together. No uniforms, ranks, dates or leaders have been fixed.

## The Seven Sages

**Established:** Ayarime is one of the **Seven Sages**, a category of beings with exceptional capabilities.

**Open:** the other six identities, whether membership is ceremonial or functional, who recognises the title, and what each Sage can do. Avoid inventing names for the six here.

## Human survivors, animals and distant networks

**Established core:** Survivors inhabit a fragmented world. Human and animal life both fall within Ayarime's protective or custodial purpose. The elevated biome requires energy maintenance; distant military/extraterrestrial networks continue to communicate through her.

**Open:** settlement politics, how communities perceive Ayarime, and whether her connection to military systems creates conflicts with her duty to civilians.

## Writing principle

Every faction should have motives, individuals and costs. The machinery of world history matters only insofar as it produces choices and consequences for beings living within it.
