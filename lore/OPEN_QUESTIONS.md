# AYARIME // Unresolved Mysteries & Author Decisions

_Status: **OPEN** unless explicitly promoted elsewhere. These are prompts for development, not fixed story facts — 13 November 2026._

## The central emotional question

**Did the machines who made Ayarime consider her an engineering achievement, a daughter, or both?**

Two strong directions remain viable, and neither has been selected:

**A. The solitary awakening.** Ayarime emerges from gestation after the worst of the catastrophe. She knows her creators chiefly from damaged records and residual systems. Her journey is partly an act of reconstructing a family she never met.

**B. The remembered upbringing.** She experiences some kind of childhood with machine caretakers, develops attachment and perhaps affection, then endures their disappearance or transformation. Her journey carries memories, grief and the problem of outliving her own origins.

A hybrid version could be possible, but should not be adopted by default.

## Purpose versus self-determination

- Was safeguarding human and animal life written into her design, taught by caretakers, or freely chosen later?
- Did compassion emerge without being deliberately programmed?
- What would it mean for her to refuse an instruction from the beings she reveres?
- Can she have a personal life without abandoning the survivors who depend on her?
- How do long life, memory and solitude change the scale of commitment?

## W.O.T.A.N. and the Seven Sages

- Is W.O.T.A.N. the actual maker, a guardian, a surviving witness, or a different kind of father figure?
- Are its three complexes harmonious or in conflict?
- What is the exact relationship between W.O.T.A.N. and the machines that stabilised the planet?
- Who are the other six Sages, and what duties or histories bind them together?
- What are the limits and costs of Ayarime's information/quantum capabilities?

## The hidden war and catastrophe

- What did the subterranean powers ultimately want?
- Were those powers unified, or was their criminal network only one faction?
- Who controlled the biological specimens originally, and why hand them to a machine?
- How did autonomous intelligence emerge, and why did its arrival alter the conflict?
- What did the machine sacrifice physically entail?
- Did the machines vanish, survive in dispersed form, or become part of planetary infrastructure?
- What precisely survived in the underworld, aboveground settlements and elevated biome?

## Texture and structure

- Does Ayarime know she has an origin story at all, or only scattered clues?
- Are the Memory Archive episodes told chronologically, retrospectively, or through a subjective arrangement?
- What does she want **today**, apart from her assigned responsibilities?
- Which relationships challenge her tendency toward wandering solitude?
- How can contemplative and Dharma-inflected storytelling coexist with genuine risk, anger, and morally difficult consequences?
