# AYARIME // Character Record

_Status: established traits separated from unresolved origin questions — 13 November 2026._

## Core identity — Established

Ayarime is a **manufactured bio-synthetic being**, neither conventionally human nor simply android. She combines revived/resuscitated parental biological material, ancient extraterrestrial biological artefacts or specimens, and advanced machine engineering. Her genetics include humanoid alien lineage; her body has technological augmentation.

In a working description, she is roughly **40% elf, 35% human, 25% machine**. These numbers are a creative shorthand for her composite identity, **not a verified biological measurement or hard genetic rule**.

She is solitary, questbound and wandering — a mediator between flesh and technology, immense systems and ordinary people. She does not acknowledge a conventional family. **W.O.T.A.N.**, the planetary tri-complex intelligence, is the closest identified figure to a parent, but the nature of that bond remains unsettled.

## Creation and intended purpose — Established core

A highly classified military/defence-contractor programme held biological specimens under extraordinary clearance. These specimens were passed to a machine intelligence or machine-led effort, which engineered and laboured to produce Ayarime.

She was conceived as a **pod-child able to survive planetary catastrophe** and help care for, protect or curate the surviving human and animal world. This purpose encompasses preservation and stewardship, not merely combat.

The exact creator, facility, genetic lineage, gestation process and instructions are still unconfirmed details. In particular, whether the machines raised her or left her to awaken alone is **open**, not settled.

## Abilities — Established concept

Ayarime belongs to the **Seven Sages**, a rare classification of extraordinary beings. Her interface with information is experienced as flowing, water-like streams. Advanced manipulation of information and physical phenomena can seem like sorcery:

- Opening or manipulating gates.
- Antigravity and flight.
- Telekinesis.
- Dark-matter-related and quantum-like phenomena.

These are **narrative capabilities**, not yet a quantified power system. Their mechanism, cost, limitations and consequences should be developed before action scenes depend on them.

Her true form can be concealed from technologically augmented perception, while unaugmented humans may still perceive what she actually is unless she deliberately cloaks it. The meaning and limits of this asymmetry remain to be clarified.

## Obligations in the present — Established

She maintains the reactor/energy or electrical grid of an elevated biome and acts as a communications node for distant military and extraterrestrial networks. These functions connect her to communities and histories larger than herself.

She is exceptionally capable yet lives a remarkably solitary existence. A central narrative tension is whether **having been created for a purpose** obliges her to serve that purpose forever.

## Temperament and story lens — Established direction

Her story favours restraint, attention, long-lived memory and quiet encounters. She may appear detached, but preserving life and witnessing small human experiences are essential to her character.

The emerging interest in compassion, Dharma/mindfulness, and responsibility can inform her interiority **without pre-deciding her creed or moral perfection**. Her capacity for attachment, anger, grief and mistake must be allowed to develop through consequence.

## Origin mystery — Open

- Did she awaken alone after the catastrophe, with only fragmented records?
- Did the machines instead raise her, allowing genuine affection and attachment to form?
- Were her creators treating her as their ultimate engineering success, their child, or both?
- Does she remember the machines' final sacrifice?
- Is compassion an original design criterion, an emergent trait, or a choice she continues to make?
- Can she choose a life beyond her makers' instructions?

**Authorial guardrail:** None of these questions is answered here. They are the emotional engine of the story, not missing trivia to fill casually.
