# AYARIME // The Illustrated Memory Archive

_Status: established story-cycle titles and motifs, with interpretive notes — 13 November 2026._

The Memory Archive is not merely an exposition dump. It is a collection of intimate, sometimes fragmented records through which the world and Ayarime's character are slowly disclosed. The illustrated treatment is atmospheric, with an **original 1990s OVA sensibility**, patient observation and emotional residue.

## Volume 001 — Letters from the Lower City

**Established:** Seven illustrated slivers, set around **Beralia-01's abandoned postal district**. Motifs include rusted mailboxes, an apparently sleeping city, a stray cat, lost or unsent correspondence, and networks of care that may persist even after their senders disappear.

One recurring philosophical image is that **the upstream feeds the downstream regardless**: material or emotional consequences travel beyond their originators.

**Narrative reading, not new canon:** undelivered letters can echo Ayarime's unanswered questions about her makers. Avoid explicitly naming their addressees or turning the symbolism into a revealed origin.

## Volume 002 — Crushed Rainforest Beans

**Established:** Twelve illustrated slivers centring on a coffee-stall encounter and associated recollections or meditations. Visual/story motifs include grep and pipedream imagery, memories concatenated like fragments of a stream, a cat, fear, craving, attachment, violence, karma and judgement, **Yama's Court**, and Ayarime's quiet return.

**Narrative reading, not new canon:** the cycle can explore the tension between observing suffering and intervening in it, or between moral law and lived mercy. Its symbolism is not a literal assertion that the cosmology must contain a specified afterlife.

## Function within the larger story

These episodes should remain meaningful even to someone who has not read the lore bible. Grand history is often most powerfully felt through seemingly negligible incidents: coffee cooling in a cup, an animal seeking shelter, a message that cannot reach its destination.

Do not overwrite established sliver scripts or images by treating this summary as the source of their exact dialogue, composition or sequence. This file is an index of themes, not a transcription of the visual works.

## Future archive protocol

For a new cycle, record the title, setting, approximate number of slivers, emotional or philosophical question, what it reveals about Ayarime, and the facts it deliberately **does not** reveal. If an episode changes the world's history, update the world bible explicitly.
