# AYARIME // Narrative Archive

This directory is the **story bible** for Ayarime and the world she inhabits. It is separate from ELF//SHELL product documentation: the character may outgrow any one application.

> **Working archive — 13 November 2026.** This material collects the author's worldbuilding conversations. It is not a completed screenplay, novel, or irrevocable canon. Everything can be refined deliberately.

## Status notation

- **Established** — directly developed or affirmed in our story discussions. Still revisable by the author.
- **Working interpretation** — a plausible synthesis, not an author's final decision.
- **Open** — an intentionally unresolved question; never turn it into canon merely by repeating it.

The particularly consequential questions — whether Ayarime remembers her creators, whether they raised her, why they made her, and how she feels about her purpose — remain **open**.

## Creative intent

The story is at once planetary science fiction and intimate travel writing: a solitary, questbound mediator between flesh and technology moves through a fractured world. The scale can be cosmic; the emotional lens often remains small — rain, coffee, a stray animal, unanswered correspondence, the people left behind. Her extraordinary power should not erase vulnerability or moral consequence.

The visual identity leans toward **original, atmospheric 1990s OVA-style animation**, while the prose moves slowly enough for place, memory and silence to matter. The work should reward long attention rather than manufacturing mystery without consequences.

## Workflow and privacy

Keep **tracked** material here for intentional review and version history. The local folder `lore/private/` is ignored by Git via [lore/.gitignore](.gitignore); it is **not** pushed to GitHub. A branch in a **public repository is public too**. Do not put secrets or unreleased story material into tracked files if it must remain confidential.

Proposed additions should cite which established notes they amend and label speculative passages as such. Avoid silently converting speculation into canon.

No code, build pipeline, runtime assets, or release configuration is changed by this archive.
