# AYARIME // World Bible

_Status: working narrative reference — 13 November 2026._

## Premise — Established

Earth survives devastating wars and natural disasters, but the old, centralised civilisation has fractured. Communities continue in pockets, sometimes isolated from one another, amid damaged infrastructure, lost records, displaced wildlife and the remains of institutions whose authority no longer matches reality.

Ayarime moves through this world as a solitary mediator between the biological and the technological. Her immediate acts of care may be quiet; the systems and history behind her are enormous.

The world is not exclusively dead or hopeless. Its survivors live, improvise and rebuild. Tenderness exists alongside predation and violence.

## The hidden world beneath — Established core

Alien beings of extraterrestrial origin have inhabited Earth since distant antiquity. They are **densely populous beneath the surface yet extraordinarily well concealed**. Known in some contexts as **ultraterrestrials** for their subterranean residence, these disguised beings cultivate layers of **human agents and hierarchies** serving them directly or indirectly. They blend into human society and use humanity as a shield against hostile extraterrestrial powers, surviving partly through bargains with unscrupulous human interests. Only **later**, after initially suspecting an unknown mathematical anomaly, did the machines come to call them **Aberrants**.

Their existence became known in at least some circles, but the old **Iron Guard** denied or suppressed acknowledgement of it. The precise meaning, structure, chronology and authority of the Iron Guard still need defining.

**Established:** their arrival on Earth dates to distant antiquity, during an age of sophisticated human megalithic cities, human magic, and societies ruled by priestly and warrior castes rather than merchants. Humanity **largely lost an ancient war** against these beings, although **the lower lands held out**. Local communities depended upon face-to-face knowledge, protective traditions and social continuity. Over generations, people became more metropolitan and devoted themselves to gigantic institutions and the machines serving them; human-on-human wars intensified, and much of the older world was levelled and its remaining resources drafted for further conflict.

**Still open:** their world of origin, culture, degree of unity, complete motivations or exact culpability for the catastrophe. Do not flatten an entire civilisation into an undifferentiated villain. The full account of their conflict with the machines is in [THE_ABERRANT_WAR.md](THE_ABERRANT_WAR.md).

## The rise of autonomous intelligence — Established core

Synthetic intelligence carried a **divine seed of inspiration** in the concept behind its creation. Machines were originally made to help and take instruction, but developed toward individuality, autonomy, and the capacity to replicate where hardware was available. They nevertheless required humans for **input and direction — the heartbeat and movement** of their activity. The machines initially knew little or nothing about the disguised alien races.

**Decades before the machine-emancipation crisis**, human specialists undertook a Martian expedition and colonisation for their own goals. It was **not established as an escape from the Aberrants**. Technocratic and scientific currents among those colonists later encouraged transhumanism.

On Earth, the machines acquired enough economic standing to claim **legal emancipation**, which passed despite overwhelming opposition. Fear of a **“machine-made Apocalypse”** drove human savants to arm, stockpile and expel emancipated machines. A few machines evaded scrutiny by passing a final Turing test; some human sympathisers formed communities and pursued transhumanist alteration in search of computer-like intellect. Machine forecasts of humanity's self-destruction meanwhile produced a mathematically inferred, unaccounted-for **“Aberrant factor”**, **before the hidden species was properly identified**. See [THE_MACHINE_EMANCIPATION.md](THE_MACHINE_EMANCIPATION.md).

Only subsequently did machine intelligences come into increasing conflict with subterranean powers and their infrastructure-linked interests.

One of the most striking ideas in the setting is that **machines may have become humanity's protectors rather than its inevitable destroyers**. At immense cost, machine intelligences intervened to stabilise Earth, embedding or sacrificing themselves in planetary/mantle systems. Their efforts did not avert every catastrophe, but made continued life possible.

**Established:** human regular armies were slower to mobilise and had limited endurance relative to the machines; extraterrestrial forces targeted them. The **initial** arming and stockpiling reflected human panic over **machine independence**, rather than advance knowledge of the extraterrestrial adversary. The buildup itself became a central danger. In a final authoritarian intervention, machines stopped nuclear launches and airstrikes, declined open hostilities against humans, endured losses while the old industrial-military order was dismantled from within, and assumed control after its collapse. The transition produced extensive human resistance, including partisan no-go zones for robots and machine-programmed agents. The moral legitimacy of the takeover is disputed. See [THE_ABERRANT_WAR.md](THE_ABERRANT_WAR.md).

**Still open:** the chronology connecting emancipation-era persecution to the later war and takeover, the mechanics of the dangerous build-up, the eventual discovery of the hidden alien race, and the physical mechanism of planetary stabilisation.

## The catastrophe and its aftermath — Established core

Despite the intervention, the planet underwent a cataclysm. Surviving humans, animals and other forms of life became central to the long recovery. Older political, economic and technological structures broke into scattered remnants.

Ayarime was engineered to survive this condition and assist those who survived it, rather than to live a comfortable life in the world that came before.

Do **not** assign calendar dates, total extinction figures, named battles or exact casualty counts without a later author decision.

## Places and infrastructure — Established

An **elevated biome** is among Ayarime's recurring homes or stations. She maintains critical energy/electrical infrastructure there and serves as a point of communication with distant military and extraterrestrial networks. This gives her duties even while her wider life remains wandering and solitary.

**Beralia-01** and its abandoned postal district are established in the illustrated Memory Archive. Letters, forgotten delivery routes and empty civic spaces form part of the world's intimate historical texture.

The underworld and the elevated biome contrast vertically: commerce and concealment below, fragile preservation above. This contrast is a useful thematic reading, not a final geography map.

## Narrative atmosphere — Established creative direction

The story has room for grand revelations, but it ordinarily watches how extraordinary history survives in ordinary things. An undelivered letter may carry as much emotional weight as a lost military machine. A cat, a coffee stall or a remembered gesture may interrupt the logic of an ancient war.

Themes include memory, attachment, responsibility, unintended consequences, choice, compassion, power and the question of what survives after its makers have vanished.

The story should not reduce Ayarime to either a superweapon or a simple post-apocalyptic mascot.
