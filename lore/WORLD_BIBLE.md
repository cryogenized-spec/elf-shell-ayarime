# AYARIME // World Bible

_Status: working narrative reference — 13 November 2026._

## Premise — Established

Earth survives devastating wars and natural disasters, but the old, centralised civilisation has fractured. Communities continue in pockets, sometimes isolated from one another, amid damaged infrastructure, lost records, displaced wildlife and the remains of institutions whose authority no longer matches reality.

Ayarime moves through this world as a solitary mediator between the biological and the technological. Her immediate acts of care may be quiet; the systems and history behind her are enormous.

The world is not exclusively dead or hopeless. Its survivors live, improvise and rebuild. Tenderness exists alongside predation and violence.

## The hidden world beneath — Established core

Subterranean, humanoid and parasitic beings inhabited the underworld long before the present era. They are sometimes provisionally called **ultraterrestrials**. A widespread clandestine trading/criminal network was associated with these beings.

Their existence became known in at least some circles, but the old **Iron Guard** denied or suppressed acknowledgement of it. The precise meaning, structure, chronology and authority of the Iron Guard still need defining.

**Not yet established:** the ultraterrestrials' original home, culture, degree of unity, complete motivations or exact culpability for the catastrophe. Do not flatten an entire civilisation into an undifferentiated villain.

## The rise of autonomous intelligence — Established core

Genuinely autonomous artificial intelligence emerged as an extraordinary and, to some parties, unexpected development. It radically redistributed power. Advanced machine intelligences came into increasing conflict with subterranean powers and their infrastructure-linked interests.

One of the most striking ideas in the setting is that **machines may have become humanity's protectors rather than its inevitable destroyers**. At immense cost, machine intelligences intervened to stabilise Earth, embedding or sacrificing themselves in planetary/mantle systems. Their efforts did not avert every catastrophe, but made continued life possible.

**Working interpretation:** the war was not simply “humans versus robots”; multiple parties, divided loyalties and conflicting kinds of intelligence shaped it. The specific military sequence and physical mechanism of planetary stabilisation remain unwritten.

## The catastrophe and its aftermath — Established core

Despite the intervention, the planet underwent a cataclysm. Surviving humans, animals and other forms of life became central to the long recovery. Older political, economic and technological structures broke into scattered remnants.

Ayarime was engineered to survive this condition and assist those who survived it, rather than to live a comfortable life in the world that came before.

Do **not** assign calendar dates, total extinction figures, named battles or exact casualty counts without a later author decision.

## Places and infrastructure — Established

An **elevated biome** is among Ayarime's recurring homes or stations. She maintains critical energy/electrical infrastructure there and serves as a point of communication with distant military and extraterrestrial networks. This gives her duties even while her wider life remains wandering and solitary.

**Beralia-01** and its abandoned postal district are established in the illustrated Memory Archive. Letters, forgotten delivery routes and empty civic spaces form part of the world's intimate historical texture.

The underworld and the elevated biome contrast vertically: commerce and concealment below, fragile preservation above. This contrast is a useful thematic reading, not a final geography map.

## Narrative atmosphere — Established creative direction

The story has room for grand revelations, but it ordinarily watches how extraordinary history survives in ordinary things. An undelivered letter may carry as much emotional weight as a lost military machine. A cat, a coffee stall or a remembered gesture may interrupt the logic of an ancient war.

Themes include memory, attachment, responsibility, unintended consequences, choice, compassion, power and the question of what survives after its makers have vanished.

The story should not reduce Ayarime to either a superweapon or a simple post-apocalyptic mascot.
